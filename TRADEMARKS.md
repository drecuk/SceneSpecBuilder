# Scene Blueprint Builder Branding Policy

The Scene Blueprint Builder name, logo, and visual identity identify the project maintained by Eugene Ch’ng. They are not licensed under Apache-2.0 except where that license or applicable law expressly permits their use.

You may:

- truthfully identify the unmodified project;
- state that another project is compatible with, built from, or based on Scene Blueprint Builder;
- retain existing attribution and notices; and
- link to the official repository.

You may not use the project name, logo, or confusingly similar branding in a way that suggests an unofficial fork, product, service, or publication is endorsed by or affiliated with the project.

Forks and modified distributions should use a distinct name and icon. They may use wording such as “based on Scene Blueprint Builder” when that statement is accurate and does not imply endorsement.

This policy does not restrict descriptive, nominative, or other uses permitted by applicable law.
