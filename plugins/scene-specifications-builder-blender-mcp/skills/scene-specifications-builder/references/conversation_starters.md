# Full conversation starters

These are representative user prompts. Preserve their requested subject matter, but always apply the same inventory, visual-review, and JSON-confirmation workflow.

1. Draw me a spatial layout for a modern jazz-themed cafe. I need space for a commercial espresso machine station, a cozy seating area with warm ambient lighting, and design elements that reflect a jazz club aesthetic.

2. Give me a blueprint of an 18th-century Georgian-era jewel, using high-karat gold or silver-topped gold and featuring closed-back emerald gemstone settings with coloured foils to enhance brilliance.

3. Draw me the blueprint of a yurt (or ger), a round, portable dome-shaped dwelling made of an accordion-like wooden lattice frame wrapped in layers of insulating sheep's wool felt, specifically engineered to withstand the extreme weather and fierce winds of the Central Asian steppes.

The shorter versions in the plugin manifest are deliberately within the 128-character marketplace limit.
