# Blueprint quality checklist

Use this checklist after image generation and before presenting Stage 1.

## Layout

- Exactly four separated panels are present.
- Panel A occupies the upper half; B, C, and D form three lower columns.
- Gutters and borders are visible; material swatches remain inside Panel D.
- No unintended fifth panel, collage overlap, watermark, or clipped content appears.

## Shared scene

- All P1 and P2 inventory items are present or explicitly disclosed as not visible.
- Asset IDs refer to the same objects in every panel.
- Major locations, counts, dimensions, silhouettes, palette, and materials agree.
- Paths, access, adjacency, and containment match the review brief.
- Historical or narrative layers are visually distinguished without becoming contradictory duplicate geometry.

## Technical views

- Panel B is unambiguously top-down and orthographic.
- Panel B contains an orientation marker, metric scale bar, major footprints, and IDs.
- Panel C is a flat elevation or section with a stated viewing direction.
- Panel C includes vertical relationships, a scale figure or dimension bar, and the approved light direction.
- Panel D contains exactly two or three key callouts and no more material spheres than actual primary materials.

## Readability and disclosure

- Short IDs and labels are legible enough to cross-reference.
- Dense generated prose is avoided.
- Visual ambiguity or image-model mistakes are listed in the review brief.
- Numeric specifications are not changed merely to imitate a visual mistake.

An image model cannot guarantee exact projection or dimensions. The approved JSON remains authoritative.
